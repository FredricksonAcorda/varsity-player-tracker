FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about athlete registration, match recording, and rankings.

How are player rankings automatically calculated?

Rankings are automatically computed by our real-time engine whenever a coach records a match. Athletes are ranked by total victories, overall match winrate percentage, and least losses within their specific sport and category. Players without recorded matches remain unranked.

Can an athlete participate in multiple sports and categories?

Yes. Athletes can be enrolled across multiple sports (e.g. Badminton Men's Singles and Mixed Doubles, or Basketball 3x3 and 5v5). Each sport and category maintains independent statistics and rankings.

How do I download my official digital athlete card?

Log in with your athlete account, navigate to "My Profile", and click "Download Card (PNG)". The application generates a high-resolution collectible varsity card formatted with your photo, rank, and stats.

Who has access to record match outcomes?

Only authorized coaches and athletic officials with Admin access can record official match scores and modify roster standings. Athlete accounts have dedicated view-only and profile-editing permissions.
